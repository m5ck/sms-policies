# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 5/13/2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MC Kuhl ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our SMS notification service.

## Information We Collect

We collect the mobile phone number(s) that the account owner manually configures within the application. We do not collect mobile numbers from any third-party source, public directory, or lead list.

We may also collect:
- Message delivery status and timestamps for operational purposes
- Application-level logs related to SMS sending

## How We Use Your Information

Mobile phone numbers and related SMS data are used solely to:
- Deliver transactional SMS notifications that the account owner has configured (for example: system alerts, deployment notifications, verification codes, or other operational messages)
- Maintain logs for debugging, troubleshooting, and service reliability

## Data Sharing

**No mobile information, including phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent records, will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes at any time.**

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade mobile information to any party for any purpose.

The only third party with access to mobile numbers is Twilio, Inc., our SMS infrastructure provider, which processes messages on our behalf under its own published privacy policy. This sharing is strictly limited to what is necessary to transmit the messages.

## Opt-Out

You may stop receiving SMS messages at any time by replying **STOP** to any message received from us. You may also remove the phone number from the application configuration to halt all messages. For assistance, reply **HELP**.

## Data Retention

Mobile numbers are retained only for as long as they remain configured in the application. Message delivery logs are retained for up to 90 days for troubleshooting purposes and are then deleted.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to protect data, including encrypted storage of credentials and access controls on application infrastructure.

## Children's Privacy

The service is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.
